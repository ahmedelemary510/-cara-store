Top Trends This Season

1. Classic Overcoats
A strong comeback for the long coat in camel, gray, and navy colors. It can be easily worn over a suit or casual outfit.

2. Dark and Warm Colors
This season is dominated by colors like: burgundy, dark olive, earthy brown, and classic black.

3. Layering
Wearing multiple layers is the strongest trend, for example: T-shirt + sweater + leather jacket.

4. Wide Leg Pants
Wide pants with loafers or boots = an elegant and modern look.

5. Leather Accessories
A thick leather belt, a men's tote bag, and a classic watch.
