Product Details

Stay classic and cool with the Vans Old Skool Maroon. The first shoe to bare the iconic sidestripe, these low-top lace-ups feature a durable suede and canvas upper in a beautiful maroon hue. To reinforce the skate-inspired look, these shoes come with re-enforced toe caps, padded collars for support and flexibility, and the signature rubber waffle outsoles for superior grip. Ideal for the woman who loves effortless street style.
